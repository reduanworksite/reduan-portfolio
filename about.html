About Me

Practical skills, taught simply — and marketing that brings customers.

I am Reduan Hossain, a Bangladesh-based Digital Marketer. My work has two sides that fit together: I help businesses become visible where their customers are already searching — on Google and Google Maps — and I teach computer skills that people can use at work every day.

Through Reduan Computer, I share Microsoft Office learning in Bangla — what Microsoft Office is, how Word, Excel and PowerPoint work, and the shortcuts that save time. Through Reduan Digital, I share SEO, Local SEO and Google Business Profile education in English for business owners and marketers.

I also build the tools I teach with. Basic Computer Learning is a free beginner course with lessons, a quiz and practice exercises, and Reduan Typing Master is a typing practice and speed-test website in English and Bangla. Both are live, free to use, and open to anyone.

I work with businesses and learners in the USA and Canada, as well as at home in Bangladesh.
